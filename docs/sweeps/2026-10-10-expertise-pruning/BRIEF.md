## Why this round exists

The owner's direction (verbatim, 2026-10-10): research "resembles a graph… the more information you get,
the easier it is to find exactly the information you need… the more you know in a field, the easier it is
to tell which information is genuinely high quality and which is just noise… Over time you can even delete
some old graphs." Rounds 1–4 tested accretion, breadth-first, connections, popularity, counterparts and
team exchange. They did NOT test four things:

- **E1 Expertise grows during the search.** How does what a searcher has learned so far change what they
  search for, which sources they trust, and what they discard? Is there measured evidence that judgments
  made EARLY in a search (relevance, source quality, what counts as noise) are worse than the same
  judgments made LATER, and that experts or good researchers go BACK and re-assess what they admitted
  early? Order effects, anchoring on early documents, belief perseverance in search.
- **E2 Pruning.** When and how do good researchers drop a line of inquiry, a belief, a source, or a whole
  frame? (Reframing; abandoning hypotheses; "kill your darlings"; discarding data that the new frame makes
  irrelevant.) Measured costs of NOT pruning, and of pruning too early.
- **E3 AI research engineers specifically.** How the best AI researchers and research engineers (frontier
  labs, top academic groups, top startups) get up to speed in a new area, decide a published result is real
  (reimplement, rerun, ablate, read the code), find rare information (code, appendices, OpenReview
  reviews, workshop papers, Discords, reading groups, DMs), and filter hype. Their own words.
- **E4 Research agents, 2026-06 → now.** What has been MEASURED (ablations, not launch posts) about which
  features of deep-research agents matter: iterative knowledge accumulation, notes/memory, context
  folding or pruning, source-quality filtering that improves as knowledge accumulates, and whether an
  elaborate harness beats a strong model in a simple loop.

Report evidence FOR and AGAINST the owner's intuition. A practice that contradicts it is worth more than
one that confirms it. Return format: the one in the round-1 BRIEF, ≤ 2,500 words. Do not write files.
